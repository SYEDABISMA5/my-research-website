ABOUT ME

My Academic Journey

I am a Political Science undergraduate at Lahore College for Women University (LCWU), with a strong interest in political research, human rights, gender equality, governance and contemporary political issues.

My academic interests focus on understanding political and social challenges through research and critical analysis. I am particularly interested in issues related to gender equality, women's rights, governance and human rights.

Through this website, I aim to share my research articles, academic work, ideas and perspectives with students, researchers and readers interested in Political Science and contemporary affairs.
